- 2026-05-18 — permissions — new "Stamp-from-scope vs validate-from-scope" subsection documenting the canonical answers to the recurring "body-vs-auth permission check" request (xAPI actor IFI, `body.authorId`, `body.customerId`). Two patterns: (1) flat fields → `fieldRules.systemManaged` + `beforeCreate` stamp from scope (server can't be spoofed because the field never reaches the DB from user input); (2) nested vendor envelopes (xAPI's `statement.actor.account.name`) → `beforeCreate` validate, fail with rich per-item context. Explains why arc deliberately does NOT ship a `requireActorMatchesAuth()` `PermissionCheck` helper (category error — auth-gate abstraction vs data validation) and contrasts with `requireOwnership` (read-side, doesn't inspect inbound body). Reinforces "fewer, deeper primitives that compose" stance.
